Sharing photos is a fundamental part of today's internet. However, files carry far more digital footprints than meet the eye.

## Essential Privacy Checklists
Before you upload any document or photo, follow these safety steps:
1. **Scrub Metadata:** Use a local-first metadata cleaner to remove EXIF headers.
2. **Review Background Details:** Double-check that background elements do not show house numbers, letters, street names, or financial documents.
3. **Anonymize Faces & Plates:** Blur or redact identifying faces, car plates, or serial markers.
4. **Prefer Secure Platforms:** Share files through platforms that strip metadata automatically, but clean them yourself first to be sure.

## The Local-First Advantage
Using browser-based cleaning utilities means your files are scrubbed securely on your computer, so there is no third-party server in the middle.
