Digital photos taken on smartphones and modern cameras automatically stamp geographic location tags (GPS coordinates) directly into the metadata headers of files.

## The Risks of Location Leaks
When you upload these photos to online marketplaces, forums, or blogs, anyone can extract the location metadata:
- **Home/Work Address Leaks:** Photos taken at home can reveal your home address.
- **Tracking & Stalking:** Multiple photos taken over time can map out your daily routines and schedules.
- **Physical Theft:** Advertising expensive items for sale with geo-tagged images shows thieves exactly where they are located.

## How to Protect Yourself
You should scrub EXIF and GPS coordinates from files before publishing them to the public web.
