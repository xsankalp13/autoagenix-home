  To calculate your burn rate, enter your total monthly operating expenses (gross spend) and your total monthly revenue (cash inflows). The calculator subtracts your revenue from your expenses to determine your net burn rate. Net Burn Rate = Total Monthly Expenses - Total Monthly Revenue. If your expenses exceed your revenue, you have a positive net burn rate (spending cash). If your revenue is greater, you are cash-flow positive.

    answer: "Gross burn is the total amount of money your startup spends each month in operating expenses, regardless of revenue. Net burn is the actual cash lost each month, calculated by subtracting your monthly revenue from your gross operating expenses (Net Burn = Gross Expenses - Revenue)."

    answer: "Burn rate dictates your company's survival timeline (runway). Understanding your burn rate helps you time your next fundraising round, decide when to hire, and know when to cut costs to extend your business operations."
